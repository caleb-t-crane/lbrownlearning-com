# What changed in your rebuild

Rebuilt from https://lbrownlearning.com/. This is the honest list of what the rebuild improved and what it simplified, so nothing is a surprise later.

## Fixed for you

- Search essentials the old site had are carried across: canonical URLs, a sitemap.xml, a robots.txt, and social preview (Open Graph) tags.
- Every form is rebuilt on Netlify Forms with a spam honeypot, so submissions arrive by email with no plugin to maintain.
- Third-party leftovers from the old site (reCAPTCHA frames, chat widgets, consent bars, tracking pixels) are not carried over.
- 96 old file links (PDFs, images) still work: each one now redirects to its new home, so links already shared or indexed do not break.
- 30 large images were converted to modern WebP at several sizes (about 4667 KB lighter) and images below the fold now load only when scrolled to. The original full-size files are kept for click-to-enlarge.
- Privacy-friendly visitor stats are included - no cookies, nothing personal stored.
- 59 click-to-open detail pop-ups from the old site were rebuilt as proper modals on the pages that open them, instead of being loaded invisibly into every page as the old plugin did.
